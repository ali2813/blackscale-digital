Cookie Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the BlackScale Digital website uses cookies and similar browser storage.

The short version

This website does not set any cookies. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one setting in your browser so it can remember whether you chose light or dark mode.

What we store in your browser

NameTypePurposeHow long
bs-themeLocal storageRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode. Set only if you use the theme button.Until you clear your browser data

This setting stays in your browser. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it does not identify you. Because it is needed only to deliver a feature you asked for, we do not ask for consent before storing it.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google. Google Fonts does not set cookies, but your browser sends Google your IP address and browser details when it fetches the fonts. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles that data.
  • Netlify. Our hosting provider delivers the site and keeps standard server logs. It does not set tracking cookies on this site.
  • Calendly. When you click a "Book a call" link, you leave our site and go to Calendly. Calendly may set its own cookies, which are covered by Calendly's cookie policy.

Managing storage

You can clear the theme setting at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings. Your browser can also block or delete cookies set by other sites you visit. If we ever add cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
