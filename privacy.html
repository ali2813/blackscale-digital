Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what personal information BlackScale Digital ("BlackScale", "we", "us") collects through this website and when you contact us or work with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number, organization and role, when you email or call us.
  • Scheduling details when you book a call through Calendly. Calendly collects the information you enter in its booking form and shares it with us so we can hold the call.
  • Project information you share while we discuss or deliver an engagement, such as descriptions of your AI systems, test accounts and documents.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Netlify, records standard request data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of request, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Font requests. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google when a page loads.
  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is stored in your own browser. It is not sent to us. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this website.

How we use information

  • To answer your questions and hold the calls you book
  • To scope, deliver and bill for services you request
  • To keep the website and our systems secure
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Data we handle during client engagements

When we test an AI system for a client, we may come across data that belongs to that client or its users. We handle that data only to perform the services, under the client's written agreement with us and the client's instructions. Our testing uses test accounts and seeded test data wherever possible. We do not keep data our agents reach during security testing beyond what is needed as evidence of a finding, and we delete engagement data when the agreement says to.

Who we share information with

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting (Netlify), scheduling (Calendly), email and file storage. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect rights, property or safety.
  • Business transfers, if BlackScale is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and scheduling information for as long as we are in touch with you and for a reasonable period after. We keep business and billing records as long as the law requires. Engagement data is kept and deleted as set out in the client agreement.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state or other privacy laws. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires.

Children

This website and our services are intended for organizations, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Links to other sites

Our site links to third-party sites such as Calendly. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date at the top of this page when we do.
