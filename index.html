    The agents

    Three agents test every system we cover. A BlackScale analyst reviews each finding before it reaches you.

    Accuracy

    Probe

    Asks your AI the questions people actually ask, and checks every answer against your own policies and documents.

    • Builds a test set from your website, policies, code and FAQs
    • Tests in every language you serve
    • Finds the outdated or conflicting documents behind wrong answers
    • Reruns the full set after every change

    Red team

    Breach

    Tries to make your AI leak data, break its own rules or give harmful advice, before someone else does.

    • Prompt injection and jailbreak attempts
    • Leaks of personal data and records
    • Impersonation of staff and officials
    • Runs only in the scope and window you approve

    Blue team

    Warden

    Checks that your AI treats people fairly, keeps watching after launch, and keeps the records.

    • Compares answers across languages, names and reading levels
    • Flags changes in behavior after a vendor or model update
    • Reviews a monthly sample of real conversations, with personal data removed
    • Keeps the evidence log for your audits and reports

    Questions teams ask first

    Do you certify that our AI is compliant?

    No. We test and document. The evidence report shows what was tested, what we found, what was fixed and when. Your counsel or auditors use it to decide whether you meet a given rule.

    We bought our chatbot from a vendor. Can you still test it?

    Yes. Most tests run through the same chat window the public uses, so we don't need the vendor's code. Findings are written so you can send them straight to the vendor.

    What will the red team agent not do?

    It never runs outside the scope and time window you approve in writing. It doesn't use real resident or client data, run tests that could take your service down, or keep any data it reaches. It stops at proof and reports.

    Which rules does the report cover?

    Every report maps results to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. We also note the state and local AI laws, accessibility rules and anti-discrimination obligations you tell us apply to you.
