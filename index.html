    The agents

    Each agent is set up for your product, runs on our infrastructure, and has a BlackScale engineer behind it.

    Core product

    Probe Reliability QA

    Probe learns your app the way a new QA hire would, by using it. Then it runs every important flow on each deploy and tells you the moment one stops working.

    • Explores signup, checkout, billing and admin flows and drafts a test plan for you to approve
    • Runs every flow in real browsers on each pull request and every night
    • Checks API responses, emails, webhooks and invoices, not only what is on screen
    • Adapts when your UI changes, and tells you each time it did
    • Files regressions with a video trace, repro steps and the deploy that caused them

    Questions teams ask first

    Do the agents need access to production?

    No. Most teams start on staging with seeded test accounts. Some later add read-only checks against production for flows like signup and checkout.

    How is this different from writing Playwright or Cypress tests?

    You don't write or maintain the tests. Probe drafts them, keeps them working as your UI changes, and checks outcomes your scripts usually skip, like the invoice total or the email that should have gone out.

    What will the red team agent not do?

    It never runs outside the targets and time window in your signed authorization. It does not run denial-of-service tests, touch real customer data on purpose, or keep any data it reaches. It stops at proof and reports.

    Where do findings show up?

    In the tools your team already uses: your issue tracker, your CI checks and a Slack or Teams channel. Every finding links to its trace.
